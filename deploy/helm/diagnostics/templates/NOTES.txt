Kubernetes diagnostics is installed at {{ .Values.appUrl }}.

   {{ .Values.appUrl }}/api/auth/callback
2. Assign users to the groups configured in your tenants.json Secret.
3. Check the deployment readiness:
   kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deployment/{{ .Release.Name }}

This chart uses one replica and local persistent storage. Do not increase replicas.
Tenant kubeconfigs require independently scoped and regularly rotated credentials.
